How UPI Giving Works

  1. Click any Donate button to open the secure donation card.
  2. Select an amount (₹10, ₹50, ₹100, ₹500, ₹1,000, or type your own amount).
  3. Scan the QR code with any UPI app on your phone (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred).
  4. Alternatively, copy our UPI ID to pay directly from your banking application.
No Hidden Charges: 100% of UPI contributions reach our verified community activities.
